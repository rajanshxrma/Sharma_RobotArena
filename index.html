How it works

Three steps to your first clash

  1. Pick a bot

    Go to the roster. Use the Tank / Speed / Support filters to find the robot that fits how you like to play.

  2. Tune it up

    In the control panel you can switch power-ups on and off and pick an AI pilot model.

  3. Check the log

    After a match, look at the battle log and open a replay to see what happened each round.
